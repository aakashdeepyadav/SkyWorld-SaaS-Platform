## Account Setup

### Step 1: Create Your Account

#### Option A: Email & Password

1. Go to [skyworld.com](https://skyworld.com)
2. Click **"Sign Up"** or **"Get Started"**
3. Select **"Sign up with Email"**
4. Fill in the form:
   - **Full Name:** Your name
   - **Email:** Your email address
   - **Password:** Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number, 1 special character)
   - **Company Name (Optional):** Your company or freelance name
5. Click **"Create Account"**

#### Option B: Google Sign-Up (Faster!)

1. Go to [skyworld.com](https://skyworld.com)
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Select **"Continue with Google"**
4. Select your Google account
5. Approve permissions
6. Done! Your profile is created automatically.

### Step 2: Verify Your Email

1. Check your email inbox for verification link from **noreply@skyworld.com**
2. Click the verification link
3. Your email is now verified! ✅

> **Tip:** If you don't see the email, check your spam folder or click "Resend OTP" on the login page.

### Step 3: Complete Your Profile

1. After login, go to **Settings** (gear icon in top-right)
2. Click **"Profile"**
3. Update your information:
   - **Profile Picture:** Upload a photo (optional)
   - **Phone Number:** Your contact number
   - **Company Name:** Your business name
   - **Industry:** Select your industry for personalized recommendations
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

✅ **You're all set!** You're now a SkyWorld client.

## Placing Your First Order

### Method 1: Buy a Fixed Service Package

**Best for:** You know exactly what you need (e.g., "I need a website")

#### Step 1: Select Your Service

1. Browse and find the service you need
2. Click **"View Details"** or **"Get Started"**
3. You'll see service tiers (e.g., Starter, Professional, Premium)

#### Step 2: Choose Your Package

Each tier includes:

- **Package Name** (e.g., "Starter Website")
- **Price** (shown in INR or USD)
- **Includes:** List of deliverables
- **Delivery Time:** Expected completion date

Click on the tier you prefer.

#### Step 3: Review Your Order

1. You'll see:
   - **Selected Package:** What you're buying
   - **Total Amount:** Price breakdown
   - **Payment Terms:** 50% advance, 50% final
   - **Delivery Timeline:** Expected date

2. Add any **Special Instructions** (optional):
   - "I prefer blue color scheme"
   - "Need it by April 15"
   - "Reference: See mycompany.com for design style"

3. Click **"Proceed to Checkout"**

#### Step 4: Payment

1. **Payment Method:**
   - Currently supports **Credit/Debit Card** and **UPI** via Razorpay
2. **Amount Charged:**
   - You pay **50% NOW** (advance payment)
   - You'll pay **50% LATER** (after initial delivery)

3. **Process:**
   - Click **"Pay Now"**
   - Razorpay secure payment modal opens
   - Enter card details (or UPI)
   - Verify OTP from your bank
   - Payment confirmation

4. **Confirmation:**
   - You'll see ✅ **"Payment Successful"**
   - Invoice emailed to your account
   - Project automatically created in your dashboard

---

### Method 2: Submit a Custom Request

**Best for:** You have a unique project that doesn't fit standard packages

#### Step 1: Go to Custom Requests

1. From dashboard, click **"Custom Requests"** in sidebar
2. Click **"Submit New Request"**

#### Step 2: Fill the Form

| Field                    | What to Enter                                         |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Project Type**         | Type of work (App Dev/Web Dev/Branding)               |
| **Project Title**        | "Mobile App for Delivery Business"                    |
| **Description**          | Detailed description of your project (2-3 paragraphs) |
| **Budget Range**         | ₹50K-₹100K (approximate)                              |
| **Timeline**             | When you need it done                                 |
| **Special Requirements** | Any specific tech/design/features needed              |

#### Step 3: Upload Files (Optional)

If you have reference materials:

- Design mockups
- Competitor websites you like
- Brand guidelines
- Wireframes
- Logo files

Click **"Add Files"** and upload.

#### Step 4: Submit

1. Review your request
2. Click **"Submit Request"**
3. You'll see confirmation: **"Request submitted successfully"**

#### Step 5: Admin Review & Pricing

- Our admin team will **review your request** within 24-48 hours
- They'll send you a **quoted price** via email
- You can **approve** or **negotiate** the quote
- Once approved, payment link is sent
- You pay and project starts!

## Communication & Collaboration

### Messaging with Your Developer

#### Sending a Message

1. Go to your **Project Detail** page
2. Scroll to **"Messages"** section
3. Type your message in the text box
4. Click **"Send"** or press Enter

#### Message Features

- **Real-time:** Messages appear instantly
- **Typing Indicators:** See when developer is typing
- **File Sharing:** Upload files directly in chat
- **Timestamps:** See when each message was sent
- **Read Receipts:** Know when developer has read your message

#### What to Communicate About

**Good examples:**

- ✅ "I like the blue color in the third design option"
- ✅ "Can we make the button bigger?"
- ✅ "I want to add a feature for user comments"
- ✅ "Can we schedule a call to discuss this?"

**Tips for better communication:**

- Be specific (e.g., "blue button on homepage" not "the button")
- Attach reference images or links
- Ask questions clearly
- Provide feedback promptly
- Schedule calls/meetings if needed

## Payments & Invoices

### Payment Schedule

SkyWorld uses a **50/50 payment split** for security:

```
Step 1: Order Placed
├─ You pay 50% (Advance payment)
├─ Project starts immediately
└─ Developer begins work

Step 2: Milestone(s) Completed
├─ Admin may request final payment
├─ You can review milestone deliverables
└─ You approve and pay remaining 50%

Step 3: Project Complete
├─ Final deliverables ready for download
├─ Invoice generated
└─ Project archived
```

### Making Payments

#### Step 1: Payment Request

You'll receive a:

- **Email notification** with payment link
- **In-app notification** in dashboard
- **Pending Payments** widget

#### Step 2: Process Payment

1. Click **"Pay Now"** button
2. Razorpay secure checkout opens
3. Enter payment method:
   - Credit Card
   - Debit Card
   - UPI (e.g., yourname@googleplay)
   - Net Banking
4. Complete 2FA verification (OTP from bank)
5. See ✅ confirmation

#### Step 3: Invoice

After successful payment:

- Invoice auto-generated
- Emailed to your account
- Available in **"Payments"** section
- Can download as PDF for accounting

---

## Account Management

### Profile Settings

#### Update Profile

1. Click **Profile** (top-right avatar)
2. Click **"Settings"**
3. Update:
   - **Name** and **Email**
   - **Phone Number**
   - **Company Info**
   - **Profile Picture**
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

#### Change Password

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click **"Security"**
3. Click **"Change Password"**
4. Enter:
   - **Current Password**
   - **New Password** (8+ chars, 1 uppercase, 1 number, 1 special char)
   - **Confirm Password**
5. Click **"Update"**

#### Reset Forgotten Password

1. On login page, click **"Forgot Password?"**
2. Enter your email
3. Click **"Send Reset Link"**
4. Check email for reset link
5. Click link: sets you to **Reset Password** page
6. Enter new password
7. Click **"Reset Password"**

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

**Recommended for extra security!**

#### Enable 2FA

1. Go to **Settings → Security**
2. Click **"Enable Two-Factor Authentication"**
3. Download an authenticator app:
   - Google Authenticator (iOS/Android)
   - Microsoft Authenticator
   - Authy
4. Scan QR code with app
5. Enter 6-digit code from app
6. Click **"Verify"**
7. Save backup codes (keep safe!)

#### Using 2FA on Login

1. Enter email and password as usual
2. Enter 6-digit code from authenticator app
3. Click **"Verify"**

### Notification Preferences

#### Customize Notifications

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications**
2. Toggle on/off for:
   - 📧 Email notifications
   - 🔔 In-app notifications
   - 💬 Message alerts
   - 📊 Project updates
   - 💳 Payment reminders

3. Click **"Save"**

### Delete Your Account

⚠️ **Warning:** This is permanent!

1. Go to **Settings → Account**
2. Scroll to bottom
3. Click **"Delete Account"**
4. Confirm deletion
5. All your data will be anonymized

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Account & Login

**Q: I can't log in. What should I do?**
A:

1. Check email spelling
2. Try "Forgot Password" to reset
3. Check if email is verified
4. Clear browser cache and try again
5. Contact support if still stuck

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes! Go to Settings → Profile → Edit Email. Verify new email then confirm.

**Q: What's the difference between email login and Google login?**
A: Both are secure. Google login is faster if you already have a Google account.
