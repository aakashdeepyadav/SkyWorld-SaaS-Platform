# SkyWorld Small Business Pricing & Profit Playbook

_Last updated: 2026-03-02_

This playbook is designed for **small and mid local businesses** (kirana, restaurants, grocery, medical, automobile, repair shops) that need digital presence at affordable pricing.

---

## 1) Positioning Goal

- Offer prices that feel affordable to local businesses.
- Keep delivery high quality using AI + reusable systems.
- Build trust, repeat clients, and recurring monthly income.
- Avoid price wars; compete on clarity, speed, and business outcomes.

## 5) Custom Combo Pricing (Negotiated)

For clients who want to **mix and match** services from different tiers, offer flexible bundled pricing.

### How it works

1. Client selects services from Package A/B/C (web) + Package D/E (branding) + Package F/G (app)
2. You calculate the bundled cost based on margin formula
3. Offer 10–15% bundle discount (so you keep 30–35% margin)
4. Both client and you agree on final price in writing

### Example combo scenarios

**Combo 1: Restaurant Starting Out**

- Package B (Starter Website): ₹7,499
- Package D (Starter Branding): ₹2,499
- Google Business setup: ₹1,500
- **Standalone total**: ₹11,498
- **Combo price (12% discount)**: ₹10,099
- **Your margin**: ~32%

**Combo 2: Medical Shop Growing**

- Package C (Growth Website): ₹11,999
- Package E (Branding Plus): ₹4,999
- 2 extra pages: ₹1,600
- Basic local SEO: ₹2,000
- **Standalone total**: ₹20,598
- **Combo price (15% discount)**: ₹17,508
- **Your margin**: ~35%

**Combo 3: Service Business Premium**

- Package C (Growth Website): ₹11,999
- Package E (Branding Plus): ₹4,999
- Package F (Mini App/PWA): ₹18,999
- Care Plan Lite (3 months): ₹4,497
- **Standalone total**: ₹40,494
- **Combo price (18% discount)**: ₹33,205

### Guardrails for custom combos

- Minimum combo margin: **30%** (don't go below)
- Maximum discount: **20%** (preserves profit)
- Scope must be signed in writing (attach detailed deliverables checklist)
- Client can customize, but payment terms remain: **50% advance, 40% preview, 10% final**

## 6) Infrastructure Costs (What You Pay, Monthly + Annual)

### Domain Registration

- **.in domain**: ₹500–₹1,200/year (cheap, local)
- **.com domain**: ₹800–₹1,500/year (premium)
- Renewal cost usually same as registration
- **Recommendation**: Buy for 2 years upfront to lock in price

### Web Hosting (Shared hosting for small business websites)

#### Budget option (recommended for starter packages)

- **Bluehost / Hostinger / SiteGround (shared)**
- Cost: ₹150–₹400/month or ₹1,500–₹4,500/year
- Includes: 1 domain free, SSL, auto-backups, 1-click WordPress
- Suitable for Package A/B/C (5–8 page sites)

#### Mid-scale option (if managing 20+ sites)

- **Cloud hosting (Linode, DigitalOcean, AWS Lightsail)**
- Cost: ₹400–₹800/month
- Includes: More control, better scalability, better uptime (99.9%+)
- Better for multiple client projects

#### App hosting (for Package F/G)

- **Render / Railway / Vercel**
- Cost: Free tier available, paid from ₹300–₹1,500/month
- Ideal for deployment without DevOps expertise

### SSL Certificates

- **Shared hosting**: Usually FREE (Let's Encrypt or bundled)

### Email Hosting (if offering client email)

## 7) How to Calculate Margin & Profit

Use this for every project:

$$
\text{Total Cost} = \text{Your Time Cost} + \text{Designer Cost} + \text{Tools} + \text{Buffer (10-15\%)}
$$

$$
\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{Total Cost}
$$

$$
\text{Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Selling Price}} \times 100
$$

### Practical cost assumptions (starter stage)

- Your internal time cost target: **₹250–₹400/hour**
- Designer support allocation: **₹500–₹2,500/project** (split with designer friend or keep if you do)
- Infrastructure allocation per project: **₹300–₹800** (hosting, domain, email, tools pro-rata)
- Rework buffer: **10–15%**

### Example margins (target)

- Package A (₹3,999): cost ₹2,200 → profit ₹1,799 → margin ~45%
- Package B (₹7,499): cost ₹4,400 → profit ₹3,099 → margin ~41%
- Package C (₹11,999): cost ₹7,200 → profit ₹4,799 → margin ~40%
- Package F (₹18,999): cost ₹12,000 → profit ₹6,999 → margin ~37%

**Healthy early-stage target**: keep gross margin between **35% and 45%**.

## 8) Payment Terms (Risk Control)

Use fixed terms to protect cash flow:

- **50% advance** before work starts
- **40% on preview/demo approval**
- **10% on final handover**

For projects above ₹25,000:

- Milestone billing (3–4 milestones)
- Pause work if payment milestone is delayed

---

## 9) Scope Rules (Avoid Profit Leakage)

- Define exact deliverables in writing.
- Include fixed revisions per package.
- Anything extra becomes a paid change request.
- Content delays from client shift delivery date.
- Third-party costs (ads, plugins, SMS gateways, app-store fees) are client-paid.

## 12) Decision Guardrails

Before accepting any project, answer:

1. Is expected margin at least 35%?
2. Is scope clearly defined and signed off?
3. Is payment schedule accepted by client?
4. Can delivery be done using reusable stack/template?

If any answer is “No”, either re-price, reduce scope, or decline.
